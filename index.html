First Aid Tips

🫀 Heart Attack
Chest pain, shortness of breath, left arm pain. Call 911 immediately. Chew aspirin if available.
🧠 Stroke (FAST)
Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911.
🩸 Bleeding
Apply firm, continuous direct pressure with clean cloth. Elevate if possible.
🔥 Burns
Cool with running water for 10-20 mins. Do not use ice or butter. Cover loosely.
